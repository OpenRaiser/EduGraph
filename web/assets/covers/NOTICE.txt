Textbook cover previews

Source repository: https://github.com/TapXWorld/ChinaTextbook
The accompanying catalog.json records the exact source revision, PDF path,
source blob, and textbook-volume mappings for each preview.

Only small cover previews are distributed here. Full textbook PDFs are not
part of this website or its downloadable EduGraph JSONL release.

Cover artwork and textbook content remain the property of their respective
publishers and rights holders. These images are not relicensed under the
Apache-2.0 code license or the CC BY-SA 4.0 EduGraph data license.

Matching is based on publisher, subject, series, grade and volume. A matching
cover does not certify the exact edition year or printing of a catalog record.
Missing or ambiguous matches use a text-only fallback in the explorer.
